material-card

Slotted container in three MD3 surface styles via variant.

Elevated

Sits on a low container surface with a soft shadow.

Filled

Highest container tone — useful for emphasized inline regions.

Outlined

Plain surface with an outline-variant border. Lowest visual weight.

Sample usage 
<material-card variant="elevated">
  <h3>Title</h3>
  <p>Body content via slot</p>
</material-card>
